# Evaluation protocols

Native LIBERO runners report original-goal success, including under a changed
instruction. Dual-goal evaluation separately reports New, Old and Other/timeout.
The same intervention can therefore have different metric meanings in the two
protocols.

See the [evaluation guide](../docs/evaluation.md) for scene assets, first-hit
rules, ordered predicates, random controls and external benchmark manifests.
The [reproduction guide](../docs/reproduction.md) maps paper evaluations to tools
and required artifacts.
